Even Stevens 3D Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Applies to the Even Stevens 3D game for Android (package app.evenstevens)

This policy explains what information the Even Stevens 3D game ("the game", "we", "us") handles, and what it does not. If you have a question, email and2icloudapp@gmail.com.

In short: the game does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising networks, and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. Purchases, reviews and the Daily Cut leaderboard are handled by Google Play. If you buy a token pack, our server keeps a record of that purchase, linked to a random ID for your copy of the game, to confirm it and to handle refunds.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, hardware device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. The game does not use the camera, microphone or location services. It contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

Game progress stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • progress such as unlocked arenas, mastery stars, obsidian token balance and unlocked tools and objects;
  • Daily Cut results and your daily streak;
  • settings such as sound volume, and which story chapters you have seen;
  • simple counters used to decide when to show the Google Play review prompt.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional packs of obsidian tokens. All purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or bank details. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When the game is installed, it creates a random install ID. It is not linked to your name, Google account, phone number or hardware, and it changes if you reinstall the game or clear its data. When you buy a pack, the game sends the install ID, the product and Google Play's purchase token to our purchase server, which asks Google Play to confirm the purchase before the pack is added. The server keeps a record of the purchase: the install ID, the product, Google's order number, the purchase time, whether it was a test purchase, and a one-way scrambled form of the purchase token.

We use these records only to deliver what you bought, to stop the same purchase being claimed twice, and to remove a pack from the game if its purchase is refunded or charged back. The server runs on Google Cloud (Firebase). The records are kept for as long as they are needed for these purposes and for our accounting obligations. They are not used for advertising or analytics and are not sold or shared, except with Google as the payment and hosting provider.

Daily Cut leaderboard (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Daily Cut score (your accuracy for that day's object) is sent to Google Play Games, which shows it on the Daily Cut leaderboard with your Play Games profile name and picture, to other players and to your Play Games friends. Your Play Games profile and sign-in are managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You control your profile's visibility and can sign out in the Google Play Games settings. We do not receive your name, email or contacts from Play Games, and we do not store leaderboard scores on our own servers. If you are not signed in, your score stays on your device.

Ratings and reviews

The game may ask you to rate it using Google Play's In-App Review feature, and the menu offers a "Rate & review" link to the game's Google Play page. Any rating or review you give is submitted to and handled by Google Play under Google's terms. We do not receive any personal information from this, other than what Google Play publicly shows on the store page.

Links to Google Play

Some buttons in the game, including "Rate & review" and the "Get it on Google Play" poster in the Mini Mart arena, open pages on Google Play. Once you leave the game, Google's privacy policy applies.

Children

Even Stevens 3D is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases require the Google Play account holder's approval, and parents can require approval for every purchase using Google Play's parental controls or Google Family Link.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent any information. Purchase records are shared only with Google, which processes payments and hosts our purchase server. Daily Cut scores you choose to submit while signed in to Google Play Games are shown on Google's leaderboard as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR in Europe or POPIA in South Africa give you rights over your personal information. The only information we hold is the purchase record described above, which is linked to a random install ID rather than to you by name. If you would like a copy of your purchase records or want them deleted, email us your Google Play order number and we will help. Deleting a record can stop us restoring or verifying that purchase. You can remove all game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. If you have any request or concern, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that handles personal information, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
